Signal type reference — what each tag means & when to use it From the Trial Intel taxonomy · click to expand
Newly Initiated
Capability-forward
Triggers whenTrial registered within the past 90 days · status RECRUITING or NOT_YET_RECRUITING
What it meansSponsor is actively picking vendors right now — CRO, sites, clinical supply, CDMO
Outreach toneAcknowledge congratulatory noise in their inbox, then pivot to the value chain and next-phase readiness. Don't lead with a generic capabilities pitch.
Phase Transition candidate
Congratulatory · benchmark
Triggers whenTrial is currently in Phase 2 or Phase 3 (candidate for an upcoming phase transition)
What it meansNew service needs are emerging — regulatory strategy, CMC scale-up, Phase 2/3 CRO, supply chain expansion
Outreach toneCongratulatory and forward-looking. Reference a named comparable trial that made the same jump — makes the outreach feel like intelligence, not a template.
Enrollment complete
Congratulatory · pre-readout
Triggers whenTrial status is ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING (enrollment done, awaiting readout)
What it meansSponsor is 6–12 months from primary readout — commercial-scale manufacturing and submission-prep decisions are being made now
Outreach toneAnticipate what they'll need next: data cleaning, stability data, submission-prep support, commercial CDMO. Frame around preparing for the next phase.
General Stagnation
Awareness · empathy
Triggers whenlastUpdatePostDate is more than 180 days ago while status is still RECRUITING (or enrollment is behind pace)
What it meansRoot cause unknown — could be manufacturing, supply chain, funding, data management, or sponsor strategy. Don't diagnose.
Outreach toneAwareness and empathy, not problem-solution pitch. "We've been following this program and wanted to reach out." Sponsors sometimes just batch-upload updates late for confidentiality reasons.
Completed — check successor
Empathetic · exploratory
Triggers whenTrial status is COMPLETED and no next-phase trial from the same sponsor is registered yet
What it meansProgram may be discontinued, deprioritized, or in-licensed. Sponsor may be reassessing development strategy.
Outreach toneAcknowledge the milestone. Explore whether alternative development pathways — partnerships, out-licensing — are being considered. Don't assume the program failed.
